Beyond chemistry, I work as a **scientific designer and visual communicator**. Over the years I've helped researchers, labs and small science-driven companies make their work look as sharp as it actually is — through presentations, illustrations, brand identities and product packaging.

## Scientific design & visual communication

I have designed and produced a wide range of scientific communication materials, aimed at improving the clarity, visual impact and accessibility of research results. This includes:

- **Modern scientific presentations** for conferences, thesis defences, and institutional communications
- **Illustrations and visual aids** that translate complex scientific concepts into something a non-specialist audience can grasp
- **Coaching and training** in scientific visual communication for researchers and students

## Visual identity for research & innovation

I've contributed to the creation and modernisation of visual identities for research structures and scientific initiatives. Selected work:

- **Redesign of an institutional logo** for a research laboratory
- **Logos for scientific and agricultural projects**
- **Visual identity systems** tailored to the needs of innovative, research-driven structures

## Packaging & product design for biopesticides

I've contributed to the graphic design of products in the **biopesticide sector**, developing coherent visual elements for their presentation and commercial valorisation. This work includes:

- **Brand logo design**
- **Product label design**
- **Packaging mockups**
- **A coherent visual identity** rolled out across all packaging materials
