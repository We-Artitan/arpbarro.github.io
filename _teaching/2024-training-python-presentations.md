Beyond my visual design work, I've also coached and trained different audiences in building **technical and communication skills**.

## What I teach

I've led trainings and coaching sessions on three connected topics:

- **Python programming** — a hands-on approach oriented toward problem-solving and task automation
- **Designing professional and scientific presentations** — making slides that actually work in a research or business context
- **Visual communication principles** — how to convey complex ideas clearly to different types of audiences

## Who I teach

These trainings have been delivered to a varied audience: students, working professionals, and people **without a scientific background**.

That diversity is what shaped my approach. Teaching researchers, engineers, and complete beginners in the same week forces you to develop a pedagogy centered on **clarity, accessibility, and adapting the content to each learner's level** — explaining the same idea three different ways until everyone in the room gets it.

## What I bring

My approach combines scientific expertise, technical skills, and visual communication. I help learners:

- **Structure their ideas** efficiently before putting anything on a slide
- **Produce presentations** that are clearer and more convincing
- **Build practical skills in Python programming**
- **Communicate complex information** in a simple, understandable way

## Why it complements my other work

This teaching experience pairs naturally with my consulting work in scientific communication and visual design: I don't just deliver finished solutions, I also **transfer the methods and skills** people need to reproduce them on their own.
