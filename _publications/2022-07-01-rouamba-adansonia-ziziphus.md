*This work was carried out as part of my Master's degree research.*

The study evaluates the phytochemical content and bioactivity of fruit pulp extracts from two trees widely used in West Africa — *Adansonia digitata* (baobab, Bombacaceae) and *Ziziphus mauritiana* (jujube, Rhamnaceae). Both species are consumed nutritionally and used in traditional medicine across the Sahel.

We screened the extracts for major secondary metabolites, then assessed their **antioxidant capacity** and their **cytoprotective activity** in a relevant cellular model, to give a pharmacological basis to the traditional uses.
